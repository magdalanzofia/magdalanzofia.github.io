About

I am an emerging fashion designer based in Berlin. I studied graphic design at an art high school, where I first became interested in visual arts, and later graduated in Fashion Design from MSKPU in Warsaw. My graduate collection received distinctions from NUMÉRO, WHITEMAD and Risk Made in Warsaw.

Everyday experiences, personal observations and the subcultures I encounter often find their way into my work. I am interested in how the world around us shapes the way we express ourselves and form our identities.

Fashion and visual arts are both part of my practice. I work with graphic design, painting and collage as I develop my own visual language. Collage lets me bring different images and ideas together and explore what happens when they meet.

Upcycling is an important part of how I approach fashion. I enjoy working with existing materials, finding new ways to use them and exploring how they can be transformed into garments.
